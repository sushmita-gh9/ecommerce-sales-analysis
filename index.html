Key business insights & recommendations
1
Revenue concentration — São Paulo (SP) alone drives R$5.1M, 38% of total revenue. Other states are significantly underpenetrated and represent a clear geographic expansion opportunity.
2
One-time buyer problem — 97% of customers purchase only once. Loyal customers (4+ orders) spend 4.8× more on average. Email retention campaigns and loyalty programs are a high-ROI opportunity.
3
Credit card dominance — 79% of revenue flows through credit cards with an average of 3.7 installments, signaling price sensitivity and opportunity for EMI promotions on high-ticket categories.
4
Strong satisfaction signal — 57.6% five-star reviews and an average score of 4.08/5 confirms strong product–market fit. Investigating the 11.4% one-star reviews could unlock further retention gains.
5
Seasonal spike in May — R$1.47M peak revenue, likely tied to Brazilian holidays. Inventory and marketing should be prepared 3–4 weeks prior to fully capture seasonal demand.
